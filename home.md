# Words live in sentences.

VocabInText teaches English vocabulary inside real, graded texts — A1 to C1.
Read a text at your level, tap any word you don't know, and the text comes
back after a day, three days, a week and two weeks. Five readings, spread
over three weeks: that is how words stay.

- 460 graded texts and 2,300 comprehension questions
- A 10,000-word dictionary with examples and pronunciation
- Meanings in Turkish, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese
- No account, no sign-up — everything stays on your phone
