VocabInText

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The short version

VocabInText does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information. Everything you read and every word you save stays on your own phone. The app shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics code.

What stays on your device

  • Which texts you have read and how many times
  • The words you tapped and saved
  • Your level, chosen language, interests and daily goal
  • Your reminder preference and the hour you picked

All of this is kept in a database on your phone. It is never uploaded. If you uninstall the app, it is gone. We cannot recover it, because we never had it.

What leaves your device

Nothing from your reading. Texts, the dictionary, questions and word audio are all built into the app. Pronunciation uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice, and reminders (including the one before a free trial ends) are scheduled on the phone itself. None of them sends anything anywhere.

Purchases. If you subscribe, or start a free trial, the purchase is handled by the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android). We use RevenueCat to check whether a subscription is active. Neither we nor RevenueCat receive your name, email address, or payment details: the stores do not share them with us. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier, your device's country and type, and the purchase record itself. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email address
  • No ads, no analytics, no usage tracking
  • No selling or sharing of data, because there is none to sell
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No reading of your contacts, photos, files, location, microphone or camera

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects nothing and shows no ads.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. To remove everything the app has stored, use Settings, Reset progress, or uninstall the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address and the date above will change.
