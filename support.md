# Support

Something not working, a word with a wrong meaning, or an idea? Write to
**[aysnaytar@icloud.com](mailto:aysnaytar@icloud.com)** — every message is read.
It helps to mention your phone model and the text or word involved.

## Common questions

**Reminders don't arrive.**
Turn on *Daily reminder* in the app's Settings. If you declined the system
prompt earlier, open your phone's Settings → Notifications → VocabInText and
allow notifications — the phone will not ask a second time.

**I can't hear the word.**
Pronunciation uses your phone's built-in English voice. Check the volume, and
on Android make sure an English text-to-speech voice is installed.

**I bought premium and reinstalled the app.**
Open *Premium* and tap *Restore purchases*. Premium is tied to your Apple ID
or Google account — no sign-up needed.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play →
Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next
renewal; premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

**How do I delete my data?**
Everything is stored only on your phone. Use Settings → *Reset all progress*
in the app, or uninstall it. We hold no copy — see the
[privacy policy](../privacy/).
